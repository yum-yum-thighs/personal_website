# Managing links

See [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) for the complete content workflow and examples.

- Homepage navigation: `js/config.js`.
- Art projects: `art/projects.json` (loaded directly by the page).
- Physics resources: `physics/links.json`, with `other` and `mine` lists.

Use a title and URL for each project/resource. Keep empty categories as `[]`.
Do not add a Rayway link.
